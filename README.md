# gcl-sample-container

🎬 https://youtu.be/nEVupfZ357o

Create a GCL codespace or resume your most recent one:

https://codespaces.new/scmlab/gcl-sample-container?quickstart=1

View and manage your existing codespaces:

https://github.com/codespaces/

**Stop** pauses the codespace and keeps your files so you can resume later. **Delete** removes the codespace and any work saved only inside it. Stopped codespaces may also be automatically deleted after their retention period.

To keep a copy of your work, right-click a `.gcl` file in the Explorer and choose **Download**. Alternatively, use your own **fork** (a copy of this repository under your GitHub account), then **commit and push** your changes to it.
